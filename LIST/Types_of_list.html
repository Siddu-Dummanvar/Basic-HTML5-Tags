Ramayan
The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic attributed to the sage Valmiki, narrating the life of Lord Rama. It describes Rama's journey to rescue his wife, Sita, from the demon king Ravana. The epic emphasizes values like dharma (duty), loyalty, and devotion. Major characters include Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman, and Ravana. It is divided into seven books (kandas) and remains a cornerstone of Indian culture and spirituality.

Mahabharat
The Mahabharata is the longest epic in the world, traditionally attributed to Vyasa, depicting the Kurukshetra war. It revolves around the rivalry between the Pandavas and the Kauravas, symbolizing the battle of good versus evil. The Bhagavad Gita, a spiritual discourse by Lord Krishna, is a part of this epic. Key themes include dharma, karma, and the complexities of human relationships. It is a profound narrative blending mythology, philosophy, and moral lessons.
